VELD HERITAGE & LEGACY

PRESERVING BACKYARD FAMILY ADVENTURE

For generations, the traditional "kettie" has been a fixture of growing up in South Africa. It taught young boys and girls the value of hand-eye coordination, precision target practice, patience, and a deep respect for the bush.

At Ketties & Goete, we are bringing this traditional art form into the 21st century. By pairing heavy-duty carbon steel forks, real polished wood scales, and fiber-optic posture sights with fully biodegradable clay ammo, we ensure the next generation can shoot safely, ecologically, and with unmatched accuracy.
