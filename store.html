VELD HERITAGE & LEGACY

PRESERVING BACKYARD FAMILY ADVENTURE

For generations, the traditional "kettie" has been a fixture of growing up in South Africa. It taught young boys and girls hand-eye coordination, precision target practice, patience, and deep respect for the bush.

At Ketties & Goete, we bring this traditional art into the 21st century with heavy-duty carbon steel forks, polished wood scales, fiber-optic posture sights, and biodegradable clay ammo.
